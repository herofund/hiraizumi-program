      Where you stay

      You live in the Hiraizumi International Sharehouse with other participants: furnished rooms with futons, a shared kitchen and a co-working space. Wi-Fi and utilities are included.

      Staff live nearby and can be reached 24 hours in an emergency. We pick you up at Ichinoseki Station when you arrive.
