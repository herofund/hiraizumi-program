Japanese & Community Project in Hiraizumi

A 1-month study program combining Japanese classes, a real project with local businesses, and career learning.

USD 2,500
  • Everything in the 2-week program
  • Team project on real issues of local companies: clarifying customer needs, organizing documents, analyzing data (no coding required)
  • Mid-term and final presentations to the town, DMO and companies
  • Career sessions: goals, Japanese job postings, reflection
  • Exchange with Japanese high school and university students
  • Certificate and grade report

      Where you stay

      You live in the Hiraizumi International Sharehouse with other participants: Japanese-style tatami rooms with futons, a shared kitchen and a co-working space. Wi-Fi and utilities are included.

      Staff live nearby and can be reached 24 hours in an emergency. We pick you up at Ichinoseki Station when you arrive.
